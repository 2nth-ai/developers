What is 2nth.ai

2nth.ai is an AI platform with 300+ skills across 12 domains — technology, business, finance, legal, data, health, marketing, education, IoT, and more. Skills are packaged expertise that AI agents use to do real work.

As a partner, you can:

  • Use skills — run AI agents that leverage the skill tree for your clients
  • Contribute skills — add your domain expertise to the tree and earn revenue share
  • Build products — create branded hubs, dashboards, and AI-powered tools on the platform
  • Sell the platform — as a premier partner, demonstrate and sell 2nth.ai to enterprise customers

2nth.io — Infrastructure layer

2nth.io is the infrastructure that runs behind 2nth.ai. Builder-tier partners get access to deploy managed infrastructure for their customers:

Infrastructure

Hosted ERPNext

Per-client ERPNext instances — opinionated for your market (warehouse, manufacturing, retail). Deployed on Hetzner, managed by 2nth.io, billed through your partnership.

Infrastructure

Hosted TwentyCRM

Group CRM for partner ecosystems. Customer, lead, and deal tracking across your client base. Deployed and managed — no DevOps needed.

Infrastructure

Cloudflare Edge

Workers AI for inference, D1 for databases, KV for sessions, R2 for storage, Queues for async. All at the edge — fast globally, data stays in-region.

Infrastructure

Custom Deployments

Need something specific? Docker containers on dedicated VMs, custom databases, specialised tooling. 2nth.io provisions and manages it — you sell it to your customers.

How to build on the platform

Option 1: Use agents directly (all tiers)

Go to 2nth.ai, sign in, and start talking to agents. No code required. Good for consultants who want AI-assisted advisory.

Option 2: Integrate via API (Starter+)

Use the 2nth.ai API to call skills from your own applications:

# Example: call a skill via API
curl -X POST https://api.2nth.ai/v1/skills/execute \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN" \
  -d '{
    "skill": "fin/cash-flow",
    "input": "Forecast Q3 revenue for a citrus farm with 8,200 tonnes"
  }'

Option 3: Build a branded hub (Designer+)

Fork the skills repo, add your domain skills, deploy to your domain. Your customers see your brand, your skills, powered by the 2nth.ai engine:

# Your branded hub
skills.yourcompany.com
  ├── Your domain skills        (your IP, your expertise)
  ├── Shared 2nth.ai skills     (300+ skills, auto-resolved)
  └── 2nth.ai agent runtime     (inference, billing, auth)

Option 4: Full platform deployment (Builder)

White-label everything. Your customers get their own workspace, their own data, their own agents — all running on 2nth.io infrastructure. You sell the platform as a product.
