The Balwin World ecosystem spans fibre connectivity, green energy, infrastructure, security (Technik), lifestyle, and community impact. This partnership brings openBIM + AI-powered smart building management to Balwin's scale — transforming how 56,000 apartments are designed, built, and operated.
