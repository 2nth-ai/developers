Ask anything. Get a Calypso answer.

The training AI knows Calypso navigation paths, configuration patterns, and the regulatory rules of each African jurisdiction. Here's a real example from a Johannesburg treasury trader.

Calypso Training AI · Standard Bank Africa
Powered by 2nth.ai · Andile
User: Trader · Johannesburg Treasury Region: South Africa · SARB Calypso v16.1 Track: Front Office Module: Trade Capture
JT
Johannesburg Trader · 09:14 AM
I'm trying to capture a 6-month USD/ZAR cross-currency basis swap in Calypso but the counterparty validation is rejecting BankCo ZA. It shows a red flag on the LEI field. How do I fix this?
AI
Calypso Training AI · 09:14 AM
That LEI validation error on a ZAR cross-currency basis swap is almost always one of two things. Let me walk you through both:
📍 Navigate: Static Data → Counterparty → BankCo ZA → Legal Entity
1. LEI not populated in counterparty master
Under the Legal Entity tab, check that the LEI_CODE field has a 20-character ISO 17442 identifier. South African reporting to DTCC/Strate requires an LEI for all counterparties since SARB FSCA Directive 2/2020.
📍 Navigate: Static Data → Counterparty → BankCo ZA → Reporting → FSCA Settings
2. LEI present but not flagged as active for ZAR derivatives
Under FSCA Settings, confirm that REPORTING_ELIGIBLE = Y and the effective date is before today's trade date.
SARB context: For USD/ZAR CCS specifically, SARB Exchange Control (BoP category T04) requires the counterparty to be an Authorised Dealer. Verify BankCo ZA has the AUTH_DEALER = Y flag set under the ZAR FX permissions panel — this is a Standard Bank-specific field added in your 2023 config uplift.
Static Data → Counterparty SARB BoP T04 FSCA Dir 2/2020 LEI · ISO 17442
JT
Johannesburg Trader · 09:17 AM
Found it — AUTH_DEALER was N. Fixed that. Now the trade captured but I'm getting a pricing error: "curve not found for ZAR-JIBAR-3M". Which curve should I use?
AI
Calypso Training AI · 09:17 AM
Good catch on the AUTH_DEALER flag. For the curve issue:
📍 Navigate: Market Data → Curves → Search: "JIBAR"
Standard Bank's curve naming convention for ZAR is SBG_ZAR_JIBAR_3M_DISC. This is the discounting curve used for ZAR legs on all CCS and IRS products. The Calypso generic name ZAR-JIBAR-3M maps to it via the product template alias — but your CCS template may be referencing the old pre-migration name.
📍 Navigate: Product Setup → Cross-Currency Swap → ZAR leg → Rate Index → Edit alias
Update the rate index alias from ZAR-JIBAR-3M to SBG_ZAR_JIBAR_3M_DISC.
Note: SARB guidance on the JIBAR transition to ZARONIA (ZAR Overnight Index Average) means new trades from Q2 2025 should prefer SBG_ZAR_ZARONIA_OIS for the ZAR floating leg. Ask your quants desk which curve convention applies to this specific trade.
Market Data → Curves JIBAR → ZARONIA Template alias mismatch

Platform architecture

Edge-native. Africa-aware. Always on.

Built on Cloudflare's global edge network — requests from Nairobi, Lagos, or Johannesburg are handled within milliseconds, with no data leaving Africa's regional Cloudflare nodes unless necessary.

Request pipeline — Calypso Training AI
1
User Query — Cloudflare Edge
Standard Bank employee submits a Calypso question. The request hits the nearest Cloudflare PoP (Johannesburg, Nairobi, Lagos, etc.) — sub-50ms to edge globally.
CF Pages · South Africa / East Africa / West Africa PoPs
2
Region & Role Context Injection — KV
Session KV lookup retrieves the user's region (e.g. ZA/SARB, KE/CBK), their Calypso role track, and their module progress. This context is prepended to every AI prompt.
Cloudflare KV · session:{sid} → role, region, track
3
Intent Classification — Workers AI
Llama 3.1 8B classifies query intent at the edge (trade capture / risk / settlement / regulatory / config) and selects the relevant Vectorize namespace for RAG retrieval.
Workers AI · @cf/meta/llama-3.1-8b-instruct · ~80ms at edge
4
Knowledge Retrieval — Vectorize RAG
Semantic search against the Calypso training corpus — Nasdaq documentation, Standard Bank config notes, SARB/CBN/CBK regulatory circulars, and Andile implementation playbooks (120K+ chunks).
Vectorize · calypso-sbg-training · top-k=8 · threshold=0.72
5
Answer Generation — Claude via AI Gateway
Claude Sonnet 4.6 generates the response using retrieved chunks + region/role context. Jurisdiction-specific regulatory flags (SARB, CBN, POPIA, etc.) are automatically included where relevant.
2nth.ai AI Gateway → Claude Sonnet 4.6 · claude-sonnet-4-6
6
Progress Tracking — D1
Every interaction is logged against the user's training record in D1 (SQLite at edge). Module completion, assessment scores, and knowledge gaps feed the adaptive learning path engine.
D1 · sbg-calypso-training · users / progress / assessments
