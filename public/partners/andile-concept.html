Architecture

HOW THE PLATFORM ROUTES AI

Every request hits a Cloudflare edge node in the client's country. A lightweight Llama model classifies the intent at zero latency. Claude handles the substantive response. Results persist in D1 (SQLite at edge) and can feed into Vectorize for RAG over your document libraries.

Why this matters for Andile: Your bank clients have data residency requirements. Because the platform runs on Cloudflare, you can deploy into a client's own Cloudflare account — data never leaves their jurisdiction. POPIA (South Africa), GDPR (EU), APRA (Australia), and RBI (India) compliance is structural, not bolted on.

Getting started

FROM BRIEF TO DEPLOYED IN DAYS

As a Build Partner with Builder-tier access, you can go from a client requirement to a deployed AI tool in days, not months.

01
Sign in to the platform
Go to 2nth.ai and sign in with albert@andilesolutions.com. You'll receive a magic link. Your Builder plan and 8M token balance are already active — no payment needed to start.
2nth.ai/join.html
02
Submit a client brief
Describe the tool you want to build in plain language. The AI Brief Analyst decomposes it into a scope across relevant pillars — software, design, or both. You'll see a token estimate and full deliverable breakdown before committing anything.
03
Review the concept and approve
The platform generates a concept spec: architecture, API contracts, database schema, and UI wireframes depending on scope. You review it, adjust if needed, and approve. Tokens are only spent on generation — transparent pricing throughout.
04
Deploy to client infrastructure
Output is deployable code and specs — straight into the client's Cloudflare account, their existing CI/CD pipeline, or hosted on 2nth.ai infrastructure. You choose based on the client's data residency requirements.
wrangler deploy → client's CF account
05
Bill the client. Keep the margin.
Andile prices and invoices the client directly. 2nth.ai charges at token cost plus platform fee. The difference is your margin. Revenue share applies when clients use the deployed AI tools — 5% back to build partners on ongoing usage.
