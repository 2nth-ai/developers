EatFitSA is a specialist dietetics practice founded by Erica Jankovich RD(SA), focused on gastrointestinal health, the gut microbiome, and evidence-based nutritional therapy. Erica connects and educates both patients and colleagues in gut health and gut disease, running patient programmes, training dietitians, and maintaining a national referral network of EatFit-approved practitioners. Her domain expertise spans IBS management, Low FODMAP protocols, inflammatory bowel disease, and the translation of clinical nutrition science into practical patient outcomes.
