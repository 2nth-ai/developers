The consultant's time problem

Paton Personnel's competitive edge is sector depth and relationship continuity — consultants who know banking, insurance, and legal cold. But a large share of their day is consumed by tasks that don't require that expertise.

⏳ Without AgileX
  • 📋 Manually reading 40–80 CVs per role, extracting key data by eye
  • 📞 Phone-screening 12–20 candidates before shortlisting 5
  • 📅 Coordinating interview slots across candidate, consultant, and hiring manager calendars
  • 📊 Building Employment Equity compliance reports in spreadsheets
  • 📝 Writing individual candidate rejection feedback at scale
  • 🔍 Manually checking accreditations (LPC, FSCA, SAICA) per role
⚡ With AgileX
  • ✅ Ava parses and ranks every CV — consultant reviews a structured shortlist
  • ✅ Grant conducts structured AI screening with auditable scorecards
  • ✅ Leo owns the full scheduling loop, zero back-and-forth for consultants
  • ✅ Iris generates EE reports automatically per role and per client
  • ✅ Elle sends empathetic, personalised rejection and offer communications
  • ✅ Accreditation checks automated at screening — flagged exceptions only

How a placement works

Consultant creates a role on AgileX. From that moment, the agents take over operational work. Human touchpoints highlighted.

Consultant creates role: "Senior Credit Risk Manager — Tier 1 Bank, Cape Town"
→ Role requirements, EE targets, seniority band, salary range captured
Ava scans inbound applications + searches Paton's 30-year candidate database
→ CVs parsed, PII masked, ranked by FRM/CFA/CISA + credit risk experience
→ Shortlist of 8 candidates delivered to consultant in 4 minutes
★ Consultant reviews shortlist, approves 6 for AI screening
Grant conducts structured screening — Basel IV, IFRS 9, SA regulatory context
→ 45-min async interview per candidate, bias-monitored, scorecard generated
→ FSCA registration verified, credit risk system experience scored
Iris analyses results, builds EE compliance report for bank client
→ Top 3 candidates benchmarked, diversity targets assessed
★ Consultant reviews Iris summary, selects 3 for client presentation
Leo schedules panel interviews with bank's credit risk head + HR
→ Candidates, consultant, and bank hiring team coordinated automatically
★ Consultant leads client panel — relationship and judgement call
Elle drafts offer letter, sends onboarding pack, handles unsuccessful feedback
→ Candidate NPS captured, placement recorded, revenue tracked

★ = Human consultant touchpoint · Everything else = AgileX agents
