Executive search is a different problem

Walker Phillip doesn't place hundreds of candidates per year — they place the right few, with full discretion, into roles that shape organisations. AI doesn't change that model. It sharpens it.

📋 Volume Recruitment
  • Dozens of active roles simultaneously
  • High CV throughput — speed is the constraint
  • Active candidates on job boards
  • Standardised screening at scale
  • AI value: throughput automation
✦ Executive Search (Walker Phillip)
  • Selective mandate portfolio, every role is strategic
  • Passive candidate mapping — they're not on job boards
  • Discretion is non-negotiable — client and candidate
  • Senior diaries are hard to coordinate
  • AI value: intelligence, reach, and executive experience

Discretion as a feature, not an afterthought

Walker Phillip conducts discreet search assignments — meaning some clients don't advertise the role, and some candidates can't be seen to be looking. The AgileX platform is designed for this.

Blind mandate handling

Ava can source and map candidates without revealing the client's identity at the outreach stage. Grant's briefing conversations are structured to gauge interest and fit before any confidential details are shared — only candidates who clear qualification thresholds receive the full role briefing.

PII masking at the prompt layer

All candidate personal data is masked before processing through AI models. Names, contact details, and identifying information are tokenised — Ava and Grant work with structured profiles, not raw personal data. Re-identification only occurs for shortlisted candidates who have given explicit consent.

Audit trail without exposure

Every AI interaction is logged in 2nth.ai's D1 audit store — but access is role-scoped. Partners see their mandates; candidates' data is never cross-pollinated across client engagements. POPIA data subject access requests are answered from the audit log automatically.

A CIO search — how it runs

Walker Phillip is retained to find a Chief Investment Officer for a Cape Town-based asset manager. The role is confidential — the incumbent hasn't been told yet.

Partner opens mandate: "CIO — Cape Town AM, AUM $2.4B, multi-asset, confidential"
→ Mandate brief, comp range (confidential), IC structure, reporting line captured
Ava maps South African + diaspora CIO / senior PM talent — passive candidates only
→ CFA Institute database + LinkedIn + fund manager directories scanned
→ 34 qualified candidates identified, PII masked, AUM experience ranked
★ Partner reviews mapped universe, selects 14 for discreet outreach
Grant conducts confidential briefing conversations — mandate described at high level
→ Genuine interest, motivation, comp expectations, and notice period captured
→ 8 interested and qualified — full mandate brief shared under NDA
Iris builds shortlist briefing packs + comp benchmarking report for client
→ Each candidate: career arc, AUM track record, comp context, strengths summary
→ Market benchmarking: CIO comp at comparable SA managers (total comp banded)
★ Partner presents 5-candidate shortlist to client — briefing packs delivered
★ Client selects 3 for IC + board-level panel interviews
Leo coordinates IC panel schedules across CEO, CIO chair, and 2 board members
→ All 3 candidates × 4 executives × 2 rounds — 24 diary slots, zero consultant admin
★ Partner leads all substantive conversations — relationship and advisory only
Elle manages offer stage, counter-offer advisory, and onboarding
→ 30-day onboarding nudges · 60-day check-in · Candidate NPS captured · Referral asked

★ = Partner touchpoint  ·  Everything else = AgileX agents  ·  Partner spends time on judgement, relationships, and advice — not logistics
