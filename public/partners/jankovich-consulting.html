Jonty Jankovich-Besan CA(SA) brings over three decades of executive leadership across international consulting, telecommunications, and fisheries & aquaculture. Currently Managing Director of Aqunion (Sea Harvest Group) and Chairman of the Harvest CEO Council representing fishing harvesters globally, Jonty's career spans 13 years in UK consulting and telecoms followed by 11 years as Managing Director of Irvin & Johnson (I&J), where he led the expansion of abalone operations and secured long-term fishing rights. He also sits on the Board of Marshall Monteagle PLC.
