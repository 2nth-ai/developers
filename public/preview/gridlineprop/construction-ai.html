The Problem

Construction Is the Least Digitalised Industry on Earth

McKinsey ranks construction second-to-last in digitalisation. Projects routinely run 80% over budget and 20 months late. The industry loses R400B annually in SA alone through rework, waste, and poor coordination. The tools exist to fix this — they're just not being used.

📄

Paper BOQs

Quantity surveyors still measure from 2D PDFs. A 200-page BOQ takes 3-4 weeks to produce manually.

📊

Spreadsheet Estimates

💬

WhatsApp Management

Site instructions by voice note. RFIs lost in chat threads. Variation orders disputed because nobody can find the record.

🚫

No Handover Data

Building completes. O&M manuals are PDFs nobody reads. No digital twin. No as-built model. Knowledge walks off site.

Construction Management

The Site Runs Itself

AI doesn't replace the site manager — it gives them superpowers. Every subcontractor's progress tracked against the programme. Every delivery logged. Every quality issue documented with photos and linked to the model.

📅

4D Programme

Construction sequence linked to the 3D model. See which elements should be built this week. AI flags when actual progress deviates from planned — before the programme slips.

Scheduling
📦

Material Tracking

BIM-extracted material quantities linked to procurement. AI tracks deliveries against requirements. Alerts when stock runs low or surplus builds up. Reduces waste by 15-25%.

Procurement
📷

Quality Capture

Photo documentation linked to model elements. Take a photo of rebar → AI identifies the element → logs against the IFC GUID → inspector signs off digitally. Full audit trail.

Quality
⚠

Safety Intelligence

AI analyses site photos for safety violations: missing PPE, unguarded edges, blocked access. Logged with location, time, and responsible subcontractor. Trend analysis identifies repeat offenders.

H&S
💰

Cost Control

Real-time cost vs. budget at element level. AI predicts final account based on current burn rate and remaining scope. Flags potential overruns 4-6 weeks before they crystallise.

Commercial
📝

Digital RFIs

Request for Information linked to model location. AI suggests answers from specification, previous RFIs, and design intent. Response time drops from 5 days to 5 hours.

Coordination

SA Compliance Automation

NHBRC enrollment documentation extracted from BIM. CIDB grading evidence generated automatically. SANS 10400 compliance checking against IFC model properties. OHS Act compliance tracked per site visit. All exportable for municipal submission.

The Open Platform

Why Open Standards Matter

Proprietary BIM tools lock your data inside one vendor's format. openBIM means your building data belongs to you — forever. Any architect, any engineer, any contractor, any facilities manager can access it with any compatible tool.

🔓

No Lock-In

IFC is an ISO standard. Your data is portable across ArchiCAD, Revit, Tekla, Allplan, and 200+ other tools.

👥

Multi-Disciplinary

Architect, structural, mechanical, electrical, QS — all working on the same model. BCF manages coordination.

🕒

Lifecycle Value

The model outlives the project. Design → construction → operations → renovation. Data compounds over decades.

🚀

AI-Ready

Structured IFC data is what AI needs. No scraping PDFs. No interpreting drawings. The model IS the data.
