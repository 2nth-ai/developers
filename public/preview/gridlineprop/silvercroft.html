Solar Intelligence ▼ details

AI predicts tomorrow's solar yield from weather data. Automatically shifts heavy loads to peak production hours. Sells excess to the grid when rates are high.

Energy
Hardware: 8-10kW solar array (Canadian Solar panels), Tesla Powerwall 2 (13.5kWh), Victron MultiPlus II inverter, CT clamp power monitoring, Envoy gateway

How it works:
• Weather API pulls 48-hour forecast (cloud cover, UV index, temperature)
• AI model predicts solar yield per hour based on panel orientation + historical data
• Load scheduler shifts geyser (2kW), pool pump (1.5kW), EV charger (7kW) to 10am-2pm peak
• Battery charges during solar surplus, discharges 5pm-10pm peak tariff
• Feed-in controller sells excess at best rate (R1.40/kWh vs R0.80 off-peak)
• Load shedding: auto-switches to battery, sheds non-essential loads, extends runtime

Water Management ▼ details

Well point and rainwater harvesting with AI-managed switching. Monitors tank levels, irrigation scheduling based on soil moisture and 7-day rainfall forecast.

Water
Hardware: 5,000L JoJo tank (rainwater), 2,500L JoJo (well point), borehole pump (0.75kW), soil moisture probes (x4 zones), float switches, flow meters, pressure sensor

How it works:
• Rainwater: roof → gutters → first-flush diverter → 5,000L tank
• Well point: borehole pump fills 2,500L tank, runs during solar peak (free power)
• AI checks 7-day rain forecast — if rain coming, delays irrigation
• Soil moisture probes per garden zone: only irrigates when soil drops below threshold
• Municipal backup: auto-opens valve if both tanks below 15%
• Usage tracking: daily litres per source (rain, well, municipal), leak detection

Comfort

Predictive Maintenance ▼ details

Monitors inverter health, battery cycles, pump pressure, geyser element resistance. Alerts you before something breaks.

Maintenance
Monitored systems:
• Inverter: input/output voltage, frequency, temperature, error codes
• Battery: cycle count, state of health, cell voltage balance, charge rate
• Borehole pump: motor current, flow rate, pressure — detects cavitation or blockage
• Geyser: element resistance trend — rising resistance = scale buildup or failing element
• Pool pump: flow rate vs pressure — detects filter blockage before it trips
• Garage door motor: cycle count, travel time trend — slowing = spring fatigue
