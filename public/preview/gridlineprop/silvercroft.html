Smart Development

AI Manages Everything You Don't Want To Think About

Each home runs on an AI layer that optimises energy, water, security, and maintenance — without you lifting a finger. The house learns your patterns and adapts.

☀

Solar Intelligence

AI predicts tomorrow's solar yield from weather data. Automatically shifts heavy loads (geyser, pool pump, EV charging) to peak production hours. Sells excess to the grid when rates are high.

Energy
💧

Water Management

Well point and rainwater harvesting with AI-managed switching. Monitors tank levels, municipal backup, irrigation scheduling based on soil moisture and 7-day rainfall forecast.

Water
🔒

Security AI

Perimeter cameras with AI person detection (not motion — fewer false alarms). Licence plate recognition at gate. Automated alerts to response company. Activity heat maps.

Security
🌡

Climate Control

Learns your temperature preferences by room and time. Pre-cools before you arrive home. Balances comfort with energy efficiency. Adapts to seasons automatically.

Comfort
🔧

Predictive Maintenance

Monitors inverter health, battery cycles, pump pressure, geyser element resistance. Alerts you before something breaks — not after. Service scheduling managed by AI.
