Drilling

Multi-spindle drilling with automatic tool change. Hole patterns from CAD, positional accuracy ±0.05mm. Aluminium, steel, stainless — the robot adjusts feed and speed per material.

FANUC M-20iB/35S

Cutting

Profile cutting with carbide end mills. Contour following from DXF/STEP files. Coolant-through tooling for heat management. Cuts up to 12mm mild steel.

CNC-grade precision

Deburring

Force-controlled deburring with compliant tooling. The robot maintains constant contact pressure regardless of part variation — consistent edge quality every time.

Force control

Inspection

Vision-guided dimensional verification. Camera checks hole positions, edge quality, surface finish. AI classifies defects and generates pass/fail report per part.

AI vision

Deployment Scenarios

WHERE THE MOBILE FACTORY GOES

Mining Sites

Drill and fabricate mounting brackets, repair plates, and structural components on site. No more shipping parts 500km from the nearest workshop. Container arrives on a flatbed, operational in 4 hours.

Remote operations

Shipyards

Cut, drill, and deburr hull plates and structural members dockside. The robot handles repetitive high-accuracy work while skilled welders focus on critical joints.

Marine fabrication

Construction Sites

Prefabricate steel connections, base plates, and brackets at the point of use. Reduce transport costs and lead times for structural steel work.

On-site fabrication

Defence & Field Ops

Deployable manufacturing for military field maintenance. Produce replacement parts from CAD files anywhere a container can be delivered. Self-contained power optional.
