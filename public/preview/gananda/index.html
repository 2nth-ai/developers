Deliverables

Five outputs. One consistent story.

The AI ensures every document tells the same story — same numbers, same narrative thread, same investment thesis. Barry ensures the story is one investors believe.

📋

Business Plan

The full narrative — business model, market, competitive positioning, team, and strategy. Every investor question answered before it's asked.

  • Executive summary & investment thesis
  • Market sizing (TAM / SAM / SOM)
  • Business model & revenue streams
  • Go-to-market strategy
  • Competitive landscape
  • Team & governance
  • Use of funds & milestones
  • Risk register & mitigants
5–10 days24–36 hours
📊

Financial Model

Three-statement integrated model. Built on Gananda's sector templates, calibrated to client actuals, stress-tested across three scenarios.

  • Revenue build-up by stream
  • P&L, balance sheet, cash flow
  • 3–5 year projections, monthly (yr 1–2)
  • Scenario analysis: base / bull / bear
  • Funding runway & drawdown schedule
  • IRR / EBITDA / MOIC metrics
  • Sensitivity on price, volume, FX
5–7 days12–24 hours
🎯

Pitch Deck

12–15 slides. Built from the business plan — not a separate document. Structured for attention-limited investor audiences.

  • Problem, opportunity, solution
  • Market size & traction
  • Business model slide
  • Financial highlights
  • Team & advisors
  • The ask — amount, use, timeline
3–5 days6–12 hours
📄

Funding Memorandum

The formal institutional document. Legally precise, structurally rigorous — written for DFIs, family offices, and private equity investment committees.

  • Transaction overview & terms
  • Investment rationale
  • Detailed financial analysis
  • Deal structure & return profile
  • Exit strategy & options
  • Due diligence summary
  • Data room index
7–14 days36–48 hours

Barry's Role

Where the expertise is applied, not replaced.

The AI handles drafting volume and structural consistency. Barry applies 20+ years of deal-side judgement to the output — the part no AI can replicate.

Investment Thesis

Barry sharpens the core investment argument — why this business, why now, why this amount. The AI structures the narrative; Barry makes it compelling to the specific investor type.

Financial Stress-Test

Every projection is checked against sector reality. Growth curves are calibrated to what the investor will believe, not just what the client hopes. Unit economics validated against comparable deals.

Deal Positioning

DFIs, PE houses, banks, and angels all evaluate deals differently. Barry calibrates the ask, the structure, and the return metrics to match what the target investor pool actually expects.

Narrative Consistency

The business plan, financial model, pitch deck, and funding memorandum must tell exactly the same story. Barry reviews the full set for any inconsistency before delivery.

Africa-First

Built for the African capital landscape

Gananda's templates and Barry's expertise are calibrated for the African funding environment — not adapted from Silicon Valley playbooks.

DFI Funding

IDC, NEF, DBSA, IFC — each has distinct documentation expectations. Gananda knows the format.

Private Equity

Pan-African PE houses, growth equity, buyout. Investment committee-ready memoranda.

Venture & Angel

Early-stage raises, SAVCA-aligned term sheets, angel network pitch formats.

Bank Finance

Commercial banks and asset-backed lenders. Credit committee documentation and covenant structures.

Cross-Border

SADC, East Africa, West Africa. Multi-jurisdiction raises with consistent documentation standards.

Impact & ESG

Impact investors and ESG-aligned funds increasingly dominating African capital flows. Gananda frames the narrative accordingly.
