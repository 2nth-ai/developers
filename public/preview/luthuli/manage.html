Platform Plan — Luthuli Lodge

Agent-first lodge management that scales from 1 lodge to enterprise. Built on 2nth.ai.

✓ Live Now
Guest Agent — WhatsApp-style booking assistant. Check availability, make reservations, get lodge info. Real-time D1 database.
Staff Agent — SMS-style assistant for Nomsa & Thandiwe. Schedules, leave requests, guest check-ins, room readiness.
Manager Dashboard — Chat + dashboard for Guy, LJ, Craig. Bookings timeline, revenue, staff coverage, maintenance.
Booking Engine — Direct & Perfect Hideaways with 20% commission logic. Conservation levy (R180/person/night). Guide allocation.
Staff Rotation — 21-on/7-off cycle, Sunday rules, leave management with approval workflow.
Email Notifications — Real emails to Guy & LJ on new bookings. Guest confirmation emails. Toggle on/off.
Calendar Export — Google Calendar & .ics download for all bookings.
D1 Database — Persistent cloud database (Cloudflare D1). Guests, bookings, staff, chat history, audit log.
Agent Learning Log — Track what guests & staff ask, what worked, what didn't. Feedback + notes.
◐ Coming Next
Email Inbox Processing — Receive & process emails from guests, Perfect Hideaways, staff. Auto-create bookings from PH confirmation emails. Thread replies to booking records.
WhatsApp Business API — Real WhatsApp integration (not simulated). Guests message the lodge number, agent responds. POPIA-compliant opt-in.
SMS Gateway — Real SMS to/from staff via Twilio or Vonage. Schedule reminders, check-in alerts, leave approvals.
Payment Processing — Deposit collection & tracking. PayFast or Stripe integration. Auto-update booking status on payment.
Google Calendar Sync — Two-way sync with Guy & LJ's Google Calendars. Bookings appear automatically.
Perfect Hideaways API — Auto-sync availability with PH portal. Rate management. Commission reconciliation.
Claude AI Upgrade — Replace rule-based agent with Claude API for natural conversation. Handle complex queries, multi-booking negotiations, special requests.
○ Future — Enterprise Scale
Multi-Property — Manage multiple lodges from one dashboard. Shared staff pool, cross-property availability, group reporting.
IoT Integration — Real-time solar, water, temperature from Victron inverter & sensors. Alerts on low battery, water, equipment failure.
OTA Channel Manager — Booking.com, Airbnb, TripAdvisor, Safari Now. Centralised rate & availability management.
Financial Reporting — P&L by month/quarter/year. VAT calculations. Conservation levy reporting to Ezemvelo. Payroll integration.
Guest CRM — Repeat guest recognition, preference tracking, birthday/anniversary reminders, loyalty programme.
Wildlife & Conservation — Sighting database, ranger coordination, anti-poaching reporting, conservation impact metrics.
Voice Agent — ElevenLabs voice AI for phone-based bookings. Guest calls, agent handles the conversation.
Inventory & Procurement — Stock tracking (food, fuel, supplies, linen). Auto-reorder thresholds. Supplier management.
Staff App — Native mobile app for housekeeping checklists, task photos, clock-in/out, training modules.
