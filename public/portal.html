How skills work

  1. Browse the skills hub Visit 2nth-skills-hub.pages.dev to explore available skills across all domains.
  2. Install into your agent Copy the skill reference into your Claude Code, Cursor, or Windsurf project. Each skill is a self-contained SKILL.md that your AI agent loads as context.
  3. Want to contribute a skill? Email craig@2nth.ai with your skill idea. The skills repo is private to protect partner IP — contributions are reviewed and merged by the 2nth.ai team.
  4. Get reviewed and published Once merged, your skill goes live on the hub and you earn 5% revenue share on usage.
